YOUR AI COACH

It suggests the work, and tells you why.

The coach reads every session you log and every form check you run. Ask how your lifts are trending and it answers from your own history with a chart of your real numbers. Ask for a tweak and it proposes a specific change, like a heavier row on Monday or a swapped exercise. Nothing changes until you tap Apply.

  • Ask from the Coach tab or straight from Home
  • Proposes swaps, load changes and new days, applied in one tap
  • Progress charts built from what you actually lifted
IN THE SET

One exercise. One tap per set.

The screen holds exactly what you are doing now: the movement, a demo loop of it, and one big button. Pick your weight, start the set, rest, start the next one. The rest timer starts itself, and you can stop it whenever you are ready.

  • Set your weight before every set
  • Rest timer starts itself, stop it any time
  • A demo loop for every movement
WORKOUT TRACKING

Every set counted, and compared.

Volume by day, this week against last, weekly targets and your calorie trend, all built from what you actually lifted. A muscle heatmap shows what you have trained, and awards for streaks, PBs and workout milestones keep the next goal in sight.

  • Sessions, reps and minutes against weekly targets
  • Top exercises and a front-and-back muscle heatmap
  • Awards for 7, 14 and 30-day streaks, PBs and milestones
FORM CHECK — OPTIONAL

And when you want it, a second pair of eyes.

Prop up your phone and step into the outline. When Capylift can see your whole body, the set starts on its own. It counts every rep and gives one short correction at a time, like “chest up, look forward”, on screen and out loud. Use it on a new movement, skip it on the ones you own. Nothing is recorded: the feed is read live, frame by frame, and never saved.

  • Counts reps and checks depth, back position and balance
  • Results feed straight back to the coach
  • Live only — no video is captured or stored
