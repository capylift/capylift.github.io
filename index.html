COACHING AND PLANS
How does the coach decide what I should do?
It starts from your onboarding answers — goal, experience, measurements, available days and kit — and generates a structured week. From then on it reads what you actually logged: loads, completed sets, missed days and any form checks you ran. Heavy sessions get a deload, skipped days get rescheduled, and new equipment unlocks new movements.
COACHING AND PLANS
Can I change the plan it gave me?
Yes, all of it. Change template swaps the whole programme; Edit weekly plan changes individual days; and inside any day you can edit sets, reps and load per exercise. Nothing is locked, and the coach adapts to your edits rather than overwriting them.
COACHING AND PLANS
Do I have to use the camera?
No. Form check is optional and the app works fully without it — plans, logging and tracking all run on what you enter yourself. Turning pose detection off in Settings simply removes the feature.
TRACKING
What does Capylift track?
Every set, rep and load you log, session length, volume by day, weekly targets, streaks, awards, and per-exercise trends over time. If you connect Apple Health or Garmin it also reads the metrics you authorise there.
TRACKING
Will a rest day break my streak?
No. Scheduled rest days count as following the plan. The streak only breaks when you skip a day the plan expected you to train.
FORM CHECK
Is my form check recorded?
No. There is no recording feature — the camera feed is read live on your device, frame by frame, and nothing is written to disk. No video file is created, so there is none to upload, share or delete. What we store is the output: rep counts, joint angles and a score per set.
FORM CHECK
The camera says it cannot see me.
It needs your whole body in frame, shot from the side, with the phone roughly at hip height. Bright backlighting behind you is the most common cause — turn so the window is in front of you rather than behind.
PRIVACY AND DATA
How do I export my data?
Settings, then Data, then Export my data. You get sessions, form-check results and your profile as CSV and JSON in one archive, emailed to your account address. Large archives can take up to an hour.
ACCOUNT AND BILLING
What happens when I delete my account?
Your training history and form-check results are erased and cannot be recovered. Export first if you want a copy — the deletion sheet offers it before you confirm.
PRIVACY POLICY · UPDATED 9 AUGUST 2026

What we collect, and what never leaves your phone.

THE SHORT VERSION
Form check reads your camera live on your own device and records nothing — there is no video file at any point. We store the numbers it produces — angles, rep counts, scores — because that is what your coach reasons about. We do not sell anything to anyone.

What we collect

Account details you give us (name, email, sign-in method), the profile figures used to size your plan (age, sex, height, weight), everything you log while training, and the numeric output of form check. If you connect a service like Apple Health or Garmin we also receive the metrics you authorise there.

What we never collect

Video or images of you. Form check reads the camera feed live on your device and writes nothing to disk — no recording is made, so no footage exists to upload, share or leak. There is no setting that changes this.

Why we hold it

To build and adjust your plan, to score your form, to run streaks and awards, and to keep your history available across your devices. Your coach reasons over this data — without it the app cannot personalise anything.

Who else sees it

Nobody outside the service. We use processors for hosting, crash reporting and email delivery, each bound to handle data only on our instructions. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. Deleting your account erases your training history and form-check results. Backups age out within thirty days. Anonymised, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you may be retained.

Your controls

Export everything we hold from Settings, then Data. Delete your account from the same place. You can disconnect any connected app at any time, and turning off pose detection stops form check entirely without affecting the rest of the app.

Children

Capylift is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, write to privacy@capylift.app and we will remove it.

Changes and contact

Material changes are announced in the app before they take effect. Questions, requests and complaints go to privacy@capylift.app.
