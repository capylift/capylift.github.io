PRIVACY POLICY · UPDATED 9 AUGUST 2026

What we collect, and what never leaves your phone.

THE SHORT VERSION
Form check reads your camera live on your own device and records nothing — there is no video file at any point. We store the numbers it produces — angles, rep counts, scores — because that is what your coach reasons about. We do not sell anything to anyone.

What we collect

Account details you give us (name, email, sign-in method), the profile figures used to size your plan (age, sex, height, weight), everything you log while training, and the numeric output of form check. If you connect a service like Apple Health or Garmin we also receive the metrics you authorise there.

What we never collect

Video or images of you. Form check reads the camera feed live on your device and writes nothing to disk — no recording is made, so no footage exists to upload, share or leak. There is no setting that changes this.

Why we hold it

To build and adjust your plan, to score your form, to run streaks and awards, and to keep your history available across your devices. Your coach reasons over this data — without it the app cannot personalise anything.

Who else sees it

Nobody outside the service. We use processors for hosting, crash reporting and email delivery, each bound to handle data only on our instructions. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. Deleting your account erases your training history and form-check results. Backups age out within thirty days. Anonymised, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you may be retained.

Your controls

Export everything we hold from Settings, then Data. Delete your account from the same place. You can disconnect any connected app at any time, and turning off pose detection stops form check entirely without affecting the rest of the app.

Children

Capylift is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, write to privacy@capylift.app and we will remove it.

Changes and contact

Material changes are announced in the app before they take effect. Questions, requests and complaints go to privacy@capylift.app.
